Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Panav Payappagoudar operates this personal portfolio website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the website, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

It is written with reference to general data-handling transparency principles under applicable frameworks — including India’s Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as well as GDPR and CCPA principles where relevant — while reflecting that this is a personal, non-commercial portfolio website.

1. Information Collected & Technical Logs

Information processed when you visit or use the website falls into three narrow categories:

  • Contact Form Submissions: When you submit the contact form, you provide your Name, Email Address, and Message Content so I can identify and reply to your inquiry (an invisible honeypot field is also present strictly to filter automated spam).
  • Security & Abuse-Prevention Telemetry: When interacting with the contact verification or submission endpoints, the backend serverless functions read the request IP address for temporary in-memory rate limiting, record redacted diagnostic logs on validation or delivery errors (storing only a partial IP prefix, timestamp, and error code — never message contents), and include the sender's IP address in the plain-text notification email sent to my inbox.
  • Infrastructure & Self-Hosted Assets: Netlify (the hosting provider) may process standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address, user-agent, and path) in edge and serverless access logs. All typography font files are self-hosted with the website, so browsing pages does not generate third-party font CDN requests.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

Based on the current implementation, this website does not load third-party analytics services, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts, and its frontend code does not write cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage entries. A minimal legacy service worker script (/sw.js) only clears obsolete caches and unregisters older registrations.

3. Proof-of-Work Verification (ALTCHA)

To prevent automated spam on the contact form without tracking cookies or image puzzles, the site uses ALTCHA, an open-source cryptographic proof-of-work mechanism. Verification spans both client and server:

  • Client-Side Computation: Your browser requests a short-lived challenge token from /.netlify/functions/altcha and computes a SHA-256 proof-of-work solution locally in memory.
  • Server-Side Validation: When the form is submitted, /.netlify/functions/submit checks the challenge signature with a server-side HMAC key, verifies expiration (10-minute validity window), and temporarily holds used challenge tokens in an ephemeral in-memory anti-replay map so solutions cannot be reused.

4. Hosting & Email Transmission

Contact submissions are processed by third-party infrastructure providers strictly to deliver your message: Netlify executes the serverless validation function, Gmail SMTP (via Nodemailer over TLS) dispatches the notification email, and Proton Mail receives the delivered correspondence at panav.p@proton.me. Each provider handles data under its respective security and retention practices. Personal information is never sold, rented, or distributed for marketing or brokerage.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

This website maintains no application database. In-memory rate-limit counters and anti-replay tokens inside serverless functions are ephemeral and expire within minutes to an hour. Messages delivered to my email inbox are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to maintain correspondence. Automated deletion is not implemented; however, you may request manual deletion of prior messages at any time by emailing panav.p@proton.me.
