About Me

My Venture

The Essentialist

Modern Form. Timeless Principle.

View Store

An independent design venture focused on creating original, minimalist apparel and goods. We believe in the power of simple, intentional design to communicate ideas and values. Our collection features concept-driven pieces—t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, and more—crafted for a modern, thoughtful aesthetic. We operate a capital-efficient, print-on-demand model, allowing us to focus purely on design quality and concept development.

My Role as Founder

I am responsible for the end-to-end process, from initial design to sale, leveraging a capital-efficient, print-on-demand model.

  • Creative Direction & Design

    Develop all original artwork, typography, and visual concepts for a range of products, including t-shirts, hoodies, and mugs, using Canva.

  • E-commerce & Platform Management

    Manage all product listings, mockups, and pricing strategy on print-on-demand platforms such as Teespring and Printify.

  • Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Sales

    Built an initial customer base from the ground up through organic marketing and personal networking, successfully monetizing my design portfolio.
